Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions business owners ask us most.

Do startups need HR services?

Yes. Even small startups benefit from clear employment documentation, workplace policies, defined roles, employee records and structured people-management processes. Establishing these early can help prevent costly problems as the business grows.

Can I outsource HR for my small business?

Yes. Businesses can outsource agreed HR functions to access professional support without employing a full-time HR manager. Services can be tailored to workforce size, business requirements and budget.

Do you provide recruitment services?

Yes. We provide recruitment support, including vacancy profiling, sourcing, screening, shortlisting and interview coordination, subject to the agreed recruitment scope.

Can you help us develop HR policies and employment contracts?

Yes. We assist businesses with HR documentation, policies, employee handbooks, employment documents and related HR structures tailored to their requirements and applicable jurisdiction.

Do you work with businesses outside Ghana?

We support Ghanaian businesses and can work with international clients on suitable remote HR consulting, documentation, recruitment coordination and people-management projects. Country-specific employment law and compliance services are subject to jurisdictional expertise and scope.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the nature of the service, workforce size, complexity, deliverables and whether the engagement is a one-time project or ongoing support. Contact us to discuss your requirements and receive a tailored proposal.

How do we get started?

Contact us with a brief description of your business and HR needs. We will discuss your requirements, identify the appropriate service and provide a proposed scope and fee.
