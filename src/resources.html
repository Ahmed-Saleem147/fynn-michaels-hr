HR Essentials for Growing Businesses

Checklist

The startup HR checklist

Before or soon after you hire your first employee, make sure you have:

  • A written employment contract for every employee
  • A job description for each role
  • A simple organisational structure showing who reports to whom
  • An employee handbook setting out key policies
  • A record for each employee, kept securely
  • A planned onboarding process for new starters

Documentation

HR documents every employer should have

Clear documentation creates consistency and protects both the business and its people. Common documents include:

  • Employment documentation and contracts
  • Employee handbook and code of conduct
  • Leave policies
  • Disciplinary and grievance procedures
  • Workplace rules
  • Onboarding and exit documentation

Advisory

Signs it's time for an HR review

An HR health check can help if your business is experiencing any of the following:

  • Unclear roles or overlapping responsibilities
  • Policies that are missing, outdated or applied inconsistently
  • Repeated employee disputes or grievances
  • Difficulty recruiting or keeping the right people
  • No clear way to measure or improve performance

Performance

Building blocks of performance management

A productive workforce needs clear expectations, measurable objectives and consistent feedback. Start with:

  • Goals for the business that each role connects to
  • Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each role
  • Regular, documented performance reviews
  • Improvement plans where performance falls short
