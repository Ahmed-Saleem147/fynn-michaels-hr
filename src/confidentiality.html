Confidentiality Statement

Your business and your people's information, handled with care.

HR work involves sensitive information about businesses and the people who work in them. Fynn-Micheals HR Solutions treats all information shared with us as confidential.

Our commitment

  • Information you share in an enquiry or during an engagement is used only to understand your needs and deliver the agreed service.
  • We do not share client or employee information with third parties without your permission, unless we are required to do so by law.
  • Employee records, investigation notes and other sensitive documents are stored securely and access is limited to those working on your engagement.
  • Grievances, disciplinary matters and workplace investigations are handled discreetly and professionally.
  • On request, we can sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share detailed business information.

Enquiries

When you contact us through the website, by email or on WhatsApp, we use your details only to respond to your enquiry and discuss how we can help. Messages sent through the website form are delivered to our inbox by a form-delivery service. You are welcome to keep your first message general and share details once we've spoken.
