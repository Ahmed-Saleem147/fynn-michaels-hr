Who We Are

HR support built for growing businesses in Ghana

Fynn-Micheals HR Solutions is an HR consultancy for startups and small businesses. Many growing companies don't have an HR professional on the team, yet they still need proper contracts, clear policies, good hiring and fair handling of staff matters.

That's the gap we fill. We set up HR for new businesses, act as an outsourced HR department for growing ones, manage recruitment, and help owners fix people-management problems when they arise.
