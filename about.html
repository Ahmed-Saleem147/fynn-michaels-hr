About Fynn-Micheals HR Solutions

People. Performance. Progress.

Who We Are

We help business owners simplify HR

Fynn-Micheals HR Solutions partners with business owners to simplify HR, strengthen workplace structures and create the foundation for sustainable growth.

As a team expands, informal arrangements can become costly problems. We help businesses put the right systems, expert guidance and practical support in place, from setting up HR for the first time to providing ongoing outsourced HR support.

We support businesses in Ghana and work with international clients on agreed remote HR consulting, recruitment coordination and documentation.

Who We Work With

We Partner With

  • Startups and early-stage businesses
  • Entrepreneurs and founders
  • Small and medium-sized enterprises
  • Growing organisations
  • Businesses establishing their HR function
  • Organisations requiring outsourced HR support
  • Companies seeking recruitment assistance
  • International businesses requiring agreed remote HR support

Whether you have two employees or a growing workforce, we can help you identify the HR support your business needs.
